# Ideas — Splendor Lab (2026-10-20)

What would make this more useful than a plain Splendor app? Each idea is marked **Build** (done) or **Skip** (why).

| # | Idea | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Turns-to-afford badge on every card in the market (a check = buy now, a number = gem-taking turns needed, a dash = can't reach) | **Build** | The core skill in Splendor is seeing what's close. Exact search over real moves, checked against brute force. |
| 2 | Toggle for the badges in Settings | **Build** | Some players want a clean table. |
| 3 | Cards tab: all 90 cards sortable by points per gem, cheapest, and win rate | **Build** | Card efficiency in one list. |
| 4 | Win rate per card (how often the buyer won, Hard vs Hard, 1,000+ games) | **Build** | Measured, not guessed. |
| 5 | First-player edge and typical game length (measured) | **Build** | Shown in the Cards tab notes. |
| 6 | Hint button: what Hard would do, and why in one line | **Build** | Study mode without leaving the game. |
| 7 | Study tab after a game: % of moves that matched Hard, points lost per move, points race chart | **Build** | Turns a loss into a lesson. |
| 8 | Measured Easy / Medium / Hard with the numbers under the level "?" | **Build** | Hard beats Easy 93%, Medium 70%; Medium beats Easy 83% (1,000 games each). |
| 9 | Cities of Splendor "Cities" variant | **Build** | Official, small rule change, big strategy change. |
| 10 | Online rooms with friends and computers (shared `_online` module) | **Build** | Tim's ask for every game. |
| 11 | Pay screen that lets you choose to spend gold instead of a gem | **Build** | Matches the real rule; auto-pay is the default. |
| 12 | Card and gem fly animations, deal-in, noble glow | **Build** | Smooth motion was asked for. |
| 13 | Trading Posts module | Skip | Adds a board and per-player powers; parked until Tim asks. Data found. |
| 14 | Strongholds module | Skip | Adds a take-that mechanic that changes the feel; parked. |
| 15 | The Orient module (extra card decks) | Skip | 30 more cards with special powers; big job, parked. Data found. |
| 16 | Hide-the-screen step between "Friend" seats on one device (so blind reserves stay secret) | Skip | Online rooms cover secret play with friends; one-device Friend seats still work, reserves shown. |
| 17 | Undo | Skip | Not in the real game; would make the Study numbers meaningless. |
| 18 | Opening book (best first 3 moves) | Skip | Not measured; the Hint button already answers it move by move. |
